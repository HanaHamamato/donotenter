How to Play — 90 Seconds

1

Camera

WASD or drag to pan • Wheel / pinch to zoom • C centers on you

2

Select

Click a territory you own. Inspect troops, terrain, buildings

3

Economy

Troops & Gold grow per territory. Cities ↑ pop, Factories ↑ army, Ports ↑ trade + navy

4

Attack Ratio

Slider chooses % of troops to commit. More = stronger but leaves you exposed. T/Y keys

5

Attack

With source selected, click a neighbor enemy/neutral. Attack travels, then battles. Reinforce to help

6

Build

On owned land, build City / Factory / Port (coastal) / Defense Post / Silo. Takes time, costs gold

7

Naval

Ports enable sea assaults to any coastal foe. Watch the ship cross water

8

Diplomacy

Ally to block friendly fire. Break to betray. Use quick chat

9

Win

Domination: control ~60% of land. Or survive timer / elimination
